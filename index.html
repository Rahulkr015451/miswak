Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Shadab
Conservative & Endodontist

My goal is simple — to provide every patient the same quality of care I would want for my own family. Dentistry should never be something you fear.

Dr. Shadab holds a BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery) and MDS (Master of Dental Surgery) with a specialization in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics. With over a decade of clinical practice, he brings precision, patience, and a gentle chairside manner to every procedure.

His expertise spans tooth-coloured restorations, laser-assisted root canal therapy, and complex smile rehabilitation. He is committed to continuing education and adopts the latest evidence-based techniques to ensure optimal outcomes for each patient.
